# Honda Research Institute USA — Research Intern: Human-AI Group Interaction

Job Number P25INT-51 · San Jose, CA · 3 months · Desired start 8/31/2026

HRI-US seeks a Human-AI Group Interaction Research Intern to study how people collaborate with AI agents in team settings. The intern supports research on AI teammates that act as peers, facilitators, leaders, or observers during collaborative tasks. The central question is how AI changes the team process: who participates, who trusts the AI, when the AI helps or disrupts, how workload shifts, whether the group reaches better shared decisions. This role is for a candidate with strong empirical HCI or human-factors research skills: design user studies, observe and analyze group interaction, interpret behavioral and survey data, and translate findings into design recommendations.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design and run user studies of human-AI group collaboration (online and/or in-person).
- Study how AI teammate roles (peer, facilitator, leader, observer) affect group interaction.
- Develop study protocols, task instructions, surveys, interview questions, participant-facing materials.
- Collect and analyze behavioral, survey, communication, and interaction-log data.
- Conduct mixed-methods analysis connecting quantitative results with qualitative observations and participant feedback.
- Identify patterns in team coordination, trust, workload, participation balance, intervention timing, group cohesion.
- Translate findings into design implications for adaptive AI teammates.
- Support pilot testing, deployment, data cleaning, visualization, research documentation.
- Contribute to internal reports, presentations, publication-oriented outputs.

## Minimum Qualifications
- MS/PhD in HCI, Human Factors, Cognitive Science, Psychology, Computer Science, Information Science, Robotics, Communication, or related.
- Experience designing, conducting, or analyzing human-subject studies, user studies, behavioral experiments, or usability studies.
- Strong empirical research methods (experimental design, survey design, behavioral measurement).
- Quantitative data analysis using Python, R, MATLAB, or similar.
- Qualitative or mixed-methods analysis (coding open-ended responses, analyzing interviews, interpreting observed interaction).
- Strong written communication; synthesize findings into clear insights.
- Research familiarity with human-AI collaboration, group interaction, AI teammates, or collaborative decision-making.

## Bonus Qualifications
- Research in human-AI / human-robot interaction, CSCW, group dynamics, team cognition, social psychology, trust in automation.
- Analyzing team interaction data (chat logs, turn-taking, intervention timing, participation patterns).
- Human factors measures (trust, workload, usability, perceived usefulness, situation awareness, team cohesion).
- Online studies using Prolific, Qualtrics, MTurk, or similar.
- Unity, JavaScript, web-based experimental systems, prototyping tools.
- Research figures, study reports, academic manuscripts; prior publication in HCI/AI/robotics/CogSci/CSCW.

Keywords: Human-AI Collaboration, HCI, Human Factors, Group Dynamics, Mixed-Methods User Research, Behavioral User Studies, AI Teammates.
