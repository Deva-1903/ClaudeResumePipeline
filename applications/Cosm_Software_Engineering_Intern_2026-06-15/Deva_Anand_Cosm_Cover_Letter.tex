\noindent Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Software Engineering internship at Cosm. A company built from Evans \& Sutherland and Spitz that now powers 12K content on a dome is exactly the kind of place where software has to meet real hardware and real audiences, and that intersection is where I most like to build. The chance to work across QA, media playback, full-stack tools, and hardware integration on systems that drive live immersive experiences is what drew me to apply.

The project closest to your team's work is Sonare, a cross-platform desktop application I built that wires camera, microphone, a frontend UI, and backend services into an on-device, real-time media pipeline. I engineered a FastAPI backend and an Electron/React frontend with async APIs and queues specifically to keep the UI responsive while handling live video and audio streams locally on the device. That experience, integrating hardware inputs, moving media through a pipeline in real time, and keeping the user-facing experience smooth, maps directly to building high-performance, real-time systems for a venue.

I also bring the engineering fundamentals the role calls for. I care about QA: I hardened a backend service with 150+ automated tests plus instrumentation, and at Wysa I added integration tests and monitored production logs during releases to catch regressions before users did. I build full-stack tools, an internal labeling application (React, Node.js, MongoDB) used daily by another team that cut their manual work by 100+ hours a month, and I work comfortably cross-functionally, which Wysa's clinical and product collaboration required every sprint. I have also built real-time streaming UX, a research assistant that streams responses to the client over WebSocket as they are generated.

I will be candid: I have not yet worked in immersive media, dome projection, or 3D graphics specifically, so that domain would be new to me. But I learn production systems fast, I am genuinely excited by what Cosm is building, and I would jump at the chance to experience the content on the dome screen and contribute to the software behind it. Thank you for considering my application.
