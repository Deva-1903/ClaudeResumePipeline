About Scowtt

Scowtt is an early-stage startup transforming the way businesses convert leads into customers through AI/ML marketing optimization and fully autonomous sales experiences. By integrating CRM, web signals, and product interaction data into a real-time conversion model, we help businesses turn interest into action—immediately. We're growing fast, and onboarding new customers quickly and cleanly is core to our success.

About the Role

We are looking for a motivated and self-driven rising senior, interested and specializing in Full-Stack Software Development to join our diverse team. In this Hybrid or remote role, you may get an opportunity to contribute to the development, deployment, and maintenance of high-quality software solutions. This is an excellent opportunity for someone eager to learn how to develop production grade Software Products in a collaborative, fast-paced environment where your unique background and ideas are valued.

What You'll Do
Design, develop, and maintain full-stack applications using Node.js, React/Next.js, and TypeScript.
Build and consume REST APIs and implement secure integrations using OAuth and industry-standard authentication practices
Collaborate with the team to integrate AI agents with external tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Twilio.
Contribute to the development of scalable solutions on AWS, utilizing technologies such as Lambda, Amplify, SQS, DynamoDB, and PostgreSQL.
Debug, optimize, and enhance application performance.
Stay up to date with emerging technologies and best practices in software development.
Follow and enforce security best practices, including secure coding, proper handling of sensitive data, authentication/authorization controls, and compliance with company security policies
