# Rippling — Software Engineer Intern, Backend Focused (Winter 2027)

## About Rippling
Rippling gives businesses one place to run HR, IT, and Finance, bringing together workforce systems (payroll, expenses, benefits, computers). Manage and automate every part of the employee lifecycle in a single system. Based in San Francisco; raised $1.4B+ from top investors.

## About the role
As an Engineering Intern, you will join one of our many teams in Winter 2027 (mid-January - April) to develop robust, well-designed products, implement new updates and features, and solve complex problems that affect our business and our clients. Rippling Interns gain the experience of full-time engineers with responsibilities, ownership, and opportunity. 13 weeks; dedicated mentor and manager.

## What you will do
- Utilize your software engineering experience to tackle important projects, solving problems, and developing quality code.
- Think critically and apply your knowledge to real-world, complex problems.
- Actively participate in team meetings to scope, drive, and progress your projects.
- Learn about the business, our clients' needs, and the impact of your team and related projects.

## What you will need
- Previous internship/Co-op experience in software engineering utilizing Python.
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's in CS or related field, graduating after the internship.
- Excellent communication skills to work cross-functionally and across departments.
- Solid programming skills with an emphasis on backend experience and knowledge.
- Ability to adapt and ask action-oriented questions to progress.
- Passion and drive to constantly learn and develop your engineering skills.

## About the team
Product teams build directly user-facing products as well as interfaces for other engineering teams, ranging from usability to scaling for massive growth. Platform engineering teams provide a layer of abstractions for every product team to build quickly and scale effortlessly, building the foundational software that powers the Rippling platform.
