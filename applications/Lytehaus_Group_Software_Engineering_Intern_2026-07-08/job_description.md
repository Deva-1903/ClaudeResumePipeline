You'd be one of the first engineers in the building.

Web and internal tools: landing pages, applicant/participant flows, production dashboards
Data pipeline work: capturing, structuring, and labeling behavioral signals from live episodes
Rapid prototyping — we ship weekly, sometimes daily, around live show schedules

Comfortable with JavaScript/TypeScript and at least one of React, Node, or Python
