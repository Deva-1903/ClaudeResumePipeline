# Gleam — Software Engineer Intern

## Company
Gleam is an iOS app helping people build confidence, social skills, and emotional intelligence through bite-sized lessons, practice conversations, and AI feedback. A consumer product at the intersection of self-improvement, education, social skills, and AI. Early, fast-moving, design-heavy; interns work on real product features that directly shape the user experience.

## Role
Software Engineer Intern, full time on-site in New York, NY. Help design, build, and ship features in our React Native app and supporting systems. Work closely with the founding team across product, engineering, and design, moving from idea to prototype to shipped feature. Best for someone who wants to build consumer software, cares about product quality, and learns quickly in a startup.

## Qualifications / What you'll do
- Build and ship product features in React Native.
- Write clean, maintainable TypeScript code.
- Work across the mobile app, front-end flows, and supporting back-end systems.
- Build APIs, integrate databases, and improve app performance.
- Debug issues and improve reliability across the product.
- Prototype new ideas quickly and iterate based on feedback.
- Contribute to UI components, design systems, animations, and interaction details.
- Participate in code reviews and product discussions.
- Own features end-to-end, from concept to implementation.

## Why Gleam
Build products people actually use. Hands-on experience shipping real features, working across engineering and design, learning how fast-moving consumer startups operate. High-growth internship for someone who wants to become a stronger engineer, better product thinker, and maybe start a company someday.
