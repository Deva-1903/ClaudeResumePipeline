# Claude Corps Fellow — CodePath × Anthropic

United States. First cohort begins October 19, 2026. Rolling review; deadline July 17, 2026 for first cohort. CodePath is employer of record. $85,000/yr + benefits, Claude API + tooling access, relocation support.

## About
Claude Corps (CodePath × Anthropic, $150M, ~1,000 fellows over time) places early-career fellows inside nonprofits, government agencies, and public-interest orgs for a year to put AI to work on problems that matter. Fellows embed full-time at a host org, build real solutions with Claude, teach the people around them, and leave behind something that keeps running.

## About the Role
Over a year, embedded at a single organization, do whatever moves the needle. Utility players who can build, teach, scope, and translate. The hard part is excellent work inside an org with stakeholders, difficult constraints, and less experience with Claude.

## Responsibilities
- Discover and scope: understand what the host actually needs; pull usage data, talk to the people doing the work, define sprint projects with clear outcomes.
- Build with Claude: ship working solutions, agents, automations, internal tools, evaluation harnesses, integrations, that make a process faster, cheaper, or newly possible.
- Enable the organization: run trainings for mixed technical and non-technical audiences; turn 3-4 colleagues into local AI go-to people. Done when someone else can own it.
- Hand off well: sit with the next owner, watch them break it, write the runbook, make it survive you.
- Exercise judgment: catch your own wrong answers; tell a stakeholder "we shouldn't build that"; cut the part of the demo that isn't landing.

## Prerequisites
Completion of both AI Fluency and Claude 101 is required; proof of completion submitted with application.

## You May Be a Good Fit If You
- 18+ with less than two years of professional work experience (care about what you've done and how you think, not your degree).
- Have built something end-to-end on your own, scoped, shipped, can talk about honestly including what you'd do differently.
- Genuine, demonstrated pull toward social-impact work (nonprofits, public service, education, health, community orgs) and can articulate why.
- Take initiative without a PM tracking you; stay coachable.
- Can explain something technical to a non-technical room and read whether it's landing.
- Energized, not deflated, by ambiguity, resource constraints, and problems different than advertised.
- Authorized to work in the US (NO visa sponsorship); open to relocation within the US for in-person work.

## Strong Candidates May Also Have
- Shipping inside a small team/org wearing multiple hats.
- Track record of teaching/mentoring/running workshops for non-technical audiences.
- Domain exposure in a host sector (public health, education, workforce, civic tech, social services).
- Experience with agent frameworks, evals, or production LLM deployments.

## What You'll Get
$85,000 salary + benefits + Claude API/tooling; relocation support (>100 mi); onboarding bootcamp with Anthropic; cohort sessions; office hours with Anthropic engineers; a portfolio entry still running six months later + references.

## Interview Process
Short application + written questions → practical take-home → 25-min conversation → final-round Super Day (two 1:1 conversations).
