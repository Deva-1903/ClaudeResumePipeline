Rippling — Engineering Intern (Full-Stack Software, Winter 2027)

About Rippling
Rippling gives businesses one place to run HR, IT, and Finance, bringing together workforce systems normally scattered across a company (payroll, expenses, benefits, computers). Based in San Francisco, raised $1.4B+. Named one of America's best startup employers by Forbes.

About the role
As an Engineering Intern, you will join one of our many teams in Winter 2027 (mid-January to April) to develop robust, well-designed products, implement new updates and features, and solve complex problems that affect our business and clients. Rippling Interns gain the experience of full-time engineers with responsibilities, ownership, and opportunity. 13 weeks. Note: this is a full stack software internship role (front end and back end).

What you will do
- Utilize your software engineering experience to tackle important projects, solving problems, and developing quality code.
- Think critically and apply your knowledge to real world, complex problems.
- Actively participate in team meetings to scope, drive, and progress your projects.
- Learn about the business, our clients' needs, and the impact of your team and related projects.

What you will need
- Previous internship/Co-op experience in software engineering, specifically in the backend with Python and the frontend using JavaScript and HTML/CSS.
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science or a related field with a graduation date after the completion of the internship.
- Excellent communication skills to work cross functionally and across departments.
- Solid frontend programming skills with an emphasis on backend experience and knowledge.
- Ability to adapt and ask action-oriented questions to progress.
- Passion and drive to constantly learn and develop your engineering skills.

About the team
Product teams build directly user-facing products as well as interfaces for other engineering teams, ranging from usability to scaling for massive growth. Platform engineering teams provide a layer of abstractions for every product team to build quickly and scale effortlessly (a "startup of startups").
