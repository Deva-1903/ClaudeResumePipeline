## About Workato AI Lab
At the forefront of enterprise AI innovation, developing cutting-edge agentic systems that transform how businesses automate and optimize workflows. Bridges academic research with real-world applications, creating AI systems that serve millions of users across global enterprises.

## Responsibilities
Seeking exceptional graduate students to join as Research Interns in SF. Work on fundamental problems in LLM-based agentic systems and efficient AI infrastructure, with opportunities to publish research while making direct impact on production systems serving enterprise customers.

- LLM Agent Systems: design and implement intelligent agent architectures for complex enterprise automation, including multi-agent collaboration, MCP, and reasoning frameworks.

- High-Performance LLM Inference: optimize inference pipelines through systems-level innovations, kernel development, and deployment strategies.

### Also responsible to
- Conduct original research on LLM agent architectures and optimization techniques.
- Develop and evaluate novel algorithms with both academic rigor and production feasibility.
- Present work at internal research seminars and external conferences.
- Mentor and collaborate with LLM engineers on implementation and deployment.
