# Notion — Software Engineer Intern

## Who We Are
Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. One place where knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side. Millions of individuals, small teams, and large companies run their work on Notion.

## About The Role
Millions of people use Notion and trust us to deliver a fast, reliable, and secure experience. You will be part of our first engineering internship class and help forge a performant and reliable path forward to Notion's future. During your 12-week internship (Sept 14 - Dec 4, SF office), you'll be paired with a mentor and work closely with the team to build and ship AI-Native projects that drive impact to customers and engineers.

## What You'll Achieve
- Write clean, secure, tested, and documented code.
- Design & enhance the Notion product with new capabilities with an AI-first mindset.
- Develop, fix and debug software across our full stack: web services, databases, applications, tools, while leveraging modern frameworks and tooling.

## Qualifications
- Pursuing a bachelor's or master's degree in CS, engineering, or related field. Must graduate before Summer 2027.
- Previous internship experience.
- Working towards proficiency in one or more languages such as TypeScript, Node.js, or Python.
- You find large challenges exciting and enjoy discovering problems as much as solving them.
- Able to problem-solve and adapt to changing priorities in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.

## Skills You'll Need to Bring
- AI enthusiast: You have built or prototyped features with AI technologies (LLMs, Embeddings, ML) and are interested in learning more.
- Thoughtful problem-solving: decompose tricky problems toward a clean solution, alone or with teammates.
- Put users first: think critically about the implications of what you're building.
- Not ideological about technology: technologies are about tradeoffs; learn new ones as you go.
- Empathetic communication; team player.

### Nice to Haves

- Familiarity with computing pioneers (Ada Lovelace, Douglas Engelbart, Alan Kay).
