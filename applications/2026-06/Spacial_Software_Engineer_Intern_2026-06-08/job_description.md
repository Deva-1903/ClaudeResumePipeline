# Spacial — Software Engineering Intern (Summer 2026)

## About Spacial
At Spacial, we're redefining how the built world gets designed, engineered, and approved. Our AI-powered platform automates structural and MEP engineering, transforming 2D plans into permit-ready, code-compliant drawings in days, not months. By connecting architects, engineers, builders, and homeowners on a single platform, Spacial eliminates friction, streamlines approvals, and accelerates how ideas become reality.
