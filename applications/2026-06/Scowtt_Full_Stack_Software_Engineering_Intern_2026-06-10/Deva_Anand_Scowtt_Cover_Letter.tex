Dear Scowtt Team,

I am applying for the Full-Stack Software Development Intern role. What caught my attention is the shape of the work: full-stack product engineering combined with AI agents that integrate real external tools to turn interest into action. That is almost exactly the kind of building I have spent the last few years doing, and I would be excited to do it on a fast-moving team.

On the full-stack side, I like shipping things that real people depend on, not class projects. I built Underdogs Fitness, a production React + Redux and Node.js platform, solo end-to-end, with JWT authentication, Stripe and manual-cash payments, and integrations with external services; it has been running a real business for over three years and was designed multi-branch from day one, so adding a second location later was a config change with zero schema migration. Before grad school I also spent two years as a backend engineer at Wysa, where I shipped a multi-tenant production backend and a full-stack React annotation tool used daily by the team, owning REST APIs, authentication and rate limiting, AWS KMS encryption, and 20+ security fixes.

The AI-agent part of your role is the piece I am most ready for. I built AgenticSearch, an AI agent that plans its own retrieval, calls external tools and APIs (web search plus LLM providers), verifies its outputs, and returns provenance for every result, hardened with 150+ automated tests; and cf-ai-research-scout, a TypeScript agent that runs multi-step tool use and streams responses to the client in real time. Wiring agents to external systems cleanly and reliably is a problem I have already enjoyed working on.

I also want to be honest about where I would be learning: I have worked in React and TypeScript but not yet Next.js, and I have used AWS (SQS, KMS) but not your full Lambda and DynamoDB stack. Those are exactly the things I am eager to pick up, and I learn fastest when I am a little out of my depth. Having worked at a small startup and delivered solo for a real client, I am comfortable owning ambiguous problems and moving quickly. Thank you for your time and consideration.
