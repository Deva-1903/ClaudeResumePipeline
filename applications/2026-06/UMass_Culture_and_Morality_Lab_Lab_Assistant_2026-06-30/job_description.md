Lab Assistant Position (Not GEO eligible)

The Culture and Morality Lab, at the Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences, is hiring a master's-level student for an hourly lab assistant position. The role is ideal for a technically strong student with experience in computational social science, website development, Python, SQL, and data science/NLP tools.

The student will help the PI (Dr. Mohammad Atari) and PhD students with technical work across lab projects, including building research websites, data-collection platforms, and project websites. The lab studies questions related to culture, morality, language, AI, and social behavior using NLP/AI methods.

Expected qualifications:

* Strong Python programming skills
* Experience with SQL and database-backed applications
* Website development experience, including front-end and/or back-end development
* Familiarity with HTML/CSS, JavaScript, Flask/Django/FastAPI, React, or similar tools
* Interest in NLP, LLM APIs, computational social science, or AI-assisted research

Position details:

Start date: As soon as possible
Hours: Variable, up to 20 hours per week
Duration: Remainder of the summer, with possible extension into the fall and spring semesters based on performance
Compensation: $29/hour

To apply, please send a CV and a one-paragraph statement of interest/fit to Dr. Atari (matari@umass.edu)

Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis until the position is filled.

This is not a GEO eligible position. A student will be hired as a grad hourly which does not provide benefits.
