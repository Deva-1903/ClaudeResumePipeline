# Meshy — Software Engineer Intern (Data Infra)

## About Meshy
Headquartered in Silicon Valley, Meshy is the leading 3D generative AI company, transforming text and images into 3D models in minutes. Team includes alumni from MIT, Stanford, Berkeley and veterans from Nvidia/Microsoft. Backed by Sequoia and GGV, raised $52M. No.1 in popularity among 3D AI tools (2024 A16Z Games), 3M monthly visits, 5M+ users, 40M models generated. Founder/CEO Yuanming (Ethan) Hu (MIT PhD, creator of Taichi GPU programming language).

## About this role
We are seeking a Software Engineer Intern to join our Data Infra team and help improve our model evaluation and visualization platform. You will act as the engineering counterpart to a cutting-edge research team, enforcing standards and building infrastructure to empower evaluation of the next generation of 3D models. You will work across backend, infrastructure, frontend, and performance. Paired with a senior engineer who owns the platform; ship real features end-to-end. AI-assisted development is the norm.

- Production databases (Postgres) and data-migration patterns

- Design and operate CI/CD pipelines (lint, type-check, tests, container builds, deploy to Kubernetes).

- Build user-facing features in collaboration with researchers and data scientists.
