## About the Team
Tesla's Autonomy Telemetry team collects and processes the data that fuels the development of Full-Self-Driving (FSD) and our Humanoid robot. Every car and robot includes a sophisticated sensor suite and internet connectivity, letting us gather data from a fleet of millions of vehicles to feed the training of our Neural Networks. The Telemetry team is responsible for the full lifecycle of this data: from specifying interesting events for data collection, to efficiently recording as much relevant data as possible on our embedded autopilot computer, to processing the data in the cloud.

- Collaborate closely with other Autonomy teams to design and implement backend components required for future Autonomy features and processes.
- Setup monitoring, metrics & reporting systems for fine-grained observability and actionable alerting, from car to cloud.
- Author bullet-proof automated tests that allow us to continue to merge and deploy with confidence.
