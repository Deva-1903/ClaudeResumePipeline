Software Engineering Intern (part-time) — Traackr

Traackr is a global SaaS technology company providing a data-driven influencer marketing platform that marketers use to optimize investments, streamline campaigns, and scale programs. Our customers range from some of the world's largest companies in the beauty and personal care space to digitally native indie brands. We are a remote-first company, with offices in San Francisco, New York, Boston, Paris, and London. Core value pillars: Trust, Diversity, Value, Ownership, Mutual success.

This position is 100% remote, with the understanding that occasional in-person attendance may be required.

We are looking for a part-time Software Engineering Intern to support internal tooling initiatives that improve reliability, visibility, and operational efficiency across our platform. This role focuses on building and enhancing internal services and automation tools used by our engineering and support teams. It's a hands-on opportunity to contribute to real systems in a short timeframe.

Responsibilities
Improve reliability and correctness of a social platform integration, including data quality fixes
Expand and validate data ingestion to support additional attributes
Automate ingestion workflows and introduce monitoring for failures
Build API endpoints to support internal services
Develop internal tools and simple UIs for data access and visibility
Identify and track key user actions across services
Contribute to monitoring, alerting, and automation of system health

Requirements
Proficiency in at least one programming language (e.g., Java, Python, Go, C++, JavaScript, Kotlin)
Basic understanding of web development concepts, including APIs and client-server architecture (CRUD, REST, databases)
Familiarity with core HTTP concepts (requests, responses, status codes)
Ability to quickly learn new technologies and work within an existing codebase
Experience with Python or Kotlin
Familiarity with data pipelines or ETL concepts
Exposure to observability tools (metrics, dashboards, alerting)
Experience working with APIs, logs, or debugging tools
Basic knowledge of databases/SQL
Basic web development knowledge and hands-on project experience
