# Joby Aviation — Software Engineer Intern (Air Taxi Systems)

Piloted eVTOL air taxi company (founded 2009), in final stages of FAA certification; launching in US and Dubai; scaling manufacturing and preparing commercial service.

## Overview
Join the Air Taxi Systems team as a Software Engineer Intern, contributing to software that brings quiet, emissions-free flight to the world. Assist in developing features across the full technology stack, including backend services, data pipelines, and implementing AI-driven features. Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and operations teams to deliver scalable software solutions from ideation to release.

## Responsibilities
- Assist in development and implementation of features across the full-stack system, including backend services and internal tools.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Product, Design, Flight Ops) to contribute to defining requirements and delivering mobile solutions.
- Assist in designing and implementing data flows for seamless app functionality.
- Contribute to client/server interactions and occasionally assist with backend APIs.
- Contribute to the implementation and integration of AI/ML models into operational software and data pipelines.
- Participate in team software processes such as requirement and test plan generation, design doc creation, code reviews and deployments.

## Required
- Currently pursuing a BS/MS in Computer Science or a related technical field
- Basic knowledge of a common modern language (e.g., Python, Go, Java).
- Familiarity with foundational concepts in data structures, algorithms, and software engineering.
- Familiarity with system design concepts, including client/server interactions.

## Desired
- Experience with a backend language (e.g., Java, Go, Python).
- Previous experience in aviation, transportation logistics, or regulated industries.
- Experience in high-growth, startup-like environments.
- Basic exposure to or coursework in machine learning, data science, or AI applications.
