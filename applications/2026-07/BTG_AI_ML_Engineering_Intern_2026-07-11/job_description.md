# BTG — AI/ML Engineering Intern

Position: AI/ML Engineering Intern. Location: Remote, United States. Job type: Internship.
Education requirement: Currently enrolled in a Master's degree program in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.

BTG is an early-stage technology company building intelligent software at the intersection of commerce, artificial intelligence, and financial technology. The company is looking for an AI/ML Engineering Intern to research, develop, test, and deploy machine-learning solutions.

## Responsibilities
- Develop, train, and evaluate machine-learning models.
- Build data pipelines for model training and evaluation.
- Work with large language models, embeddings, vector search, and retrieval systems.
- Analyze structured and unstructured datasets.
- Integrate AI models into production applications and APIs.
- Monitor and improve model performance.
- Research emerging AI technologies and practical use cases.
- Collaborate with engineers throughout the development lifecycle.
- Document technical findings and model improvements.

## Qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a Master's degree program.
- Hands-on AI/ML experience through research, internships, employment, or major technical projects.
- Strong Python skills. Experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, or similar frameworks.
- Experience with LLMs, NLP, embeddings, vector databases, RAG, or recommendation systems.
- Understanding of model development and evaluation. Experience with APIs and backend services.
- MongoDB or other NoSQL databases. Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and independent-working abilities.

## Preferred
- AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure. Model deployment. MLOps. Docker or Kubernetes. Startup experience. Open-source contributions, publications, or certifications.
