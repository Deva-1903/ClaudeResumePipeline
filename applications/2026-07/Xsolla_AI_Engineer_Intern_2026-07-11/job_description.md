Position: AI-First Engineering Intern. Compensation: $20-$25 per hour.

Xsolla is a global commerce company that provides tools and services for video-game developers to fund, distribute, market, and monetize games. Xsolla is working toward becoming an AI-first company and is hiring AI engineering interns across multiple regions.

The internship focuses on: internal AI initiatives; AI tooling and integrations; proof-of-concept applications built on Xsolla APIs and SDKs; production work for internal teams, game developers, and players.

Responsibilities:
- Partner with senior engineers to identify where AI tooling can remove friction.
- Build applications and integrations using Xsolla APIs and SDKs.
- Ship software that reaches real internal or external users.
- Use Claude Code, Cursor, or similar agentic coding tools as the default development workflow.
- Participate in weekly 30-second demonstrations showing how AI was used to solve a problem.
- Work with limited process and independently turn context into working software.
- Collaborate with senior engineers and interns across regions.

What Xsolla is looking for:
- A record of independently building software. Side projects, hackathons, open-source work, or other shipped products.
- Practical experience using Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, or similar agentic coding tools.
- Ability to explain how AI was used and how the resulting work was validated.
- Bias toward action in ambiguous situations. Comfort developing modern full-stack software.
- Current undergraduate students, graduate students, and recent graduates in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related programs may apply.

Application requirements: GitHub profile, deployed project, or another example of something built; a two-to-three-sentence answer (or two-minute video) to "What's something hard you built recently, and why did you build it?" The company evaluates proof of work rather than formal exercises.
