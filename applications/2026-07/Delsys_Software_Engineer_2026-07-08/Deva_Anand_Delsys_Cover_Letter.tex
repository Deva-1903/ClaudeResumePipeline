I am applying for the Software Engineer role on the Product Development team. A role centered on the back-end architecture behind signal acquisition and processing software, for instruments that turn real physiological signals into something researchers can trust, is exactly the kind of work I want to do.

The day-to-day of this role maps closely to what I have already built. At Wysa I designed and owned the back-end architecture of a multi-tenant clinical platform, and at Cario I created, integrated, and tested more than 20 RESTful APIs with object-oriented service design, clear contracts, and backward-compatible changes used in production. I care about the parts your posting emphasizes: sound, vetted software, clean data-management schemas, and components that are tested before they ship. On my own I hardened a Python backend service with over 150 automated tests before I trusted it.

The part of your work I am most drawn to is real signals and real devices. I built Sonare (https://github.com/Deva-1903/Qualcomm-Sep25-Team-Sonare), a real-time on-device application that interfaces directly with camera and microphone hardware and processes live audio and video streams through an on-device pipeline, with async queues that keep it responsive under continuous input. That experience of wiring software to physical sensors and keeping a real-time signal path stable is the closest thing I have to what your instruments do, and it is the direction I want to go deeper in. I have also shipped and maintained a full application end to end (https://www.underdogsfitness.in/, live for over three years).

I want to be straight about one thing: my production fluency is in Node.js, TypeScript, and Python rather than a systems language, though I have working knowledge of C++ and a strong object-oriented foundation. What I can promise is that I pick up new languages and stacks quickly and take real ownership once I do, and a systems-language backend for scientific instruments is a challenge I would be glad to grow into. Thank you for your time and consideration.
