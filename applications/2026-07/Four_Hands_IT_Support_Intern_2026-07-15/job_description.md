The IT Support Intern will assist in diagnosing and resolving hardware, software, and network issues to ensure seamless IT operations. Support system maintenance, user account management, and documentation of technical solutions.

This position is expected to start in September 2026 and continue through the entire Fall 2026 term (through December 2026). Our internships are a minimum of 12 weeks, part-time (20 to 25 hours) and on-site.

In This Role

Manage IT tickets by troubleshooting basic issues, documenting solutions, and escalating complex problems to the IT team
Follow established IT processes, suggest workflow improvements, and help create internal and user-facing documentation
Provide timely follow-ups with end users on open tickets and communicate resolutions to meet service level agreements
Assist with the offboarding process by deactivating accounts, backing up data, and tracking company assets
Help create and deploy system images for hardware setups
Maintain and organize inventory records for IT assets, including computers, peripherals, and software licenses
Track and document the lifecycle of IT equipment
Other duties as assigned, in accordance with training and qualifications

The Ideal Person

Currently pursuing a degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field
Basic understanding of Windows OS, exposure to Microsoft Azure is a plus
Familiarity with imaging tools and inventory management software
