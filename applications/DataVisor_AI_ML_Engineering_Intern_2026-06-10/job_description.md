# DataVisor — AI/ML Engineering Intern

## About DataVisor
DataVisor is the world's leading AI-powered Fraud and Risk Platform. Open SaaS platform; fraud and AML solutions that scale infinitely and act on fast-evolving fraud/money-laundering in real time. Patented unsupervised ML, device intelligence, decision engine, investigation tools. Adopted by many Fortune 500 companies.

## Role Summary
Seeking soon-to-graduate MS/PhD students in CS, ML, Data Science, or related fields as AI/ML Engineering Interns. Help build the Intelligence Layer and Data Consortium that power DataVisor's real-time fraud detection platform. Focus: distributed systems, data pipelines, ML infrastructure, and applied AI, including exposure to agentic flows and LLMs.

## What You'll Do
- Data Engineering & Pipelines: build and maintain high-throughput data pipelines using Spark, Kafka, or Flink; process and aggregate real-time signals (device fingerprints, behavioral data) into shared intelligence systems.
- Distributed Systems & Scalability: design and optimize backend systems for large-scale, real-time decisioning; improve performance, reliability, and latency under high transaction volumes.
- AI Applications & Agentic Flows: develop AI applications and agentic workflows using state-of-the-art LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google); experiment with natural language interfaces, intelligent rule suggestions, and automated strategy generation.
- Machine Learning Pipelines: deploy and monitor pipelines for unsupervised and supervised ML models; integrate models into real-time scoring APIs and decision engines.
- Privacy & Security: privacy-first system design, including tokenization and hashing to protect sensitive data.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration: work with Data Science, Product, and Engineering to test ideas, validate models, ship production features.

## Requirements
- Current MS or PhD students in CS, ML, AI, Data Science, or related field.
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Familiarity with at least one of: distributed systems, machine learning, data engineering, or backend development.
- Academic or project experience with big data frameworks (Spark, Kafka, Flink) is a plus.
- Understanding of core ML concepts (supervised / unsupervised learning).

## Preferred (Nice-to-Have)
- LLMs, RAG architectures, LangChain, or vector databases.
- Cloud platforms (AWS) and containers (Docker).
- Stream processing or real-time systems.
- Interest in fraud, risk, or security domains (not required).

Comp Range: $25 - $70/hour.
