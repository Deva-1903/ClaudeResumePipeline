# Palantir — Software Engineer / Forward Deployed Software Engineer Intern (Palantir Year)

## A World-Changing Company
Palantir builds the world's leading software for data-driven decisions and operations. Our platforms empower partners to develop lifesaving drugs, forecast supply chain disruptions, locate missing children, and more.

## Palantir Year
Surging demand from undergrads who want to get into the arena faster. Come join us for a year. Do real, critical work with the engineers and customers building the future.

## The Role
Build software and deliver customer outcomes. You'll help customers deploy AI-powered solutions, build the software platforms of the future, and transform how organizations operate. Held to the same performance standards as any Palantir engineer; progression based purely on merit and impact.

Two internship role options:

### Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE)
Embedded directly with customers to tackle their toughest challenges. FDSEs rapidly understand customer issues; architect and build solutions leveraging business-critical data and the latest AI. As an FDSE Intern, you'll experience startup autonomy with the resources/mentorship/stability of an established company, treated as a full member of the engineering team, with significant freedom and ownership. Work in small, agile teams and own end-to-end execution of high-stakes projects.

### Software Engineer
Build software at scale to transform how organizations use data. Contribute high-quality code directly to Palantir Gotham, Foundry, or Apollo. Create features used by research scientists, aerospace engineers, intelligence analysts, and economic forecasters. Product Development is made up of small teams of Software Engineers, each focusing on a specific aspect of a product.

## What We Value
- Exceptional students willing to do work that matters. We care about how you think and what you can build, not just your resume.
- Familiarity with data structures, storage systems, cloud infrastructure, front-end frameworks, and other technical tools.
- For USG: an active U.S. security clearance, or eligibility/willingness to obtain one, is required. For all other roles: a plus but not required.
- Open to freshmen and sophomores, as well as juniors and seniors in college.

## What We Require
- Pursuing or received a degree in Engineering, preferably CS, Mathematics, Software Engineering, Physics, or Data Science.
- Proficiency with one or more programming languages, such as Python, Java, C++, TypeScript/JavaScript, or similar.
- Hands-on experience applying AI (coursework, personal projects, internships, or relevant extracurriculars).
