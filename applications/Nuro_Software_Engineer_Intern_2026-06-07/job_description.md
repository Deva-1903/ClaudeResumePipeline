Nuro — Software Engineering Intern

Who We Are
Nuro is a self-driving technology company on a mission to make autonomy accessible to all. Founded in 2016, Nuro is building the world's most scalable driver, combining cutting-edge AI with automotive-grade hardware. Nuro licenses its core technology, the Nuro Driver, to support a wide range of applications, from robotaxis and commercial fleets to personally owned vehicles.

About the Role
As a software engineering intern, you will work closely with leading experts in the field of machine learning, robotics, and software. Depending on your skill sets and areas of interest, you will work on some or all of the following: Data Platform, Onboard Systems, ML Infrastructure, Simulation, or Technical Infrastructure teams.

About the Work
- Data Platform: comprehensive management system for Nuro AI Driver's data, labels, and metrics. Data annotation across domains (2D/3D perception, mapping, behavior trajectory, language/text), data ingestion and mining (heuristics and embedding search), autonomy evaluation infrastructure with detailed introspection.
- Onboard Systems: reliable, high-performance platform allowing autonomy teams to integrate their software/algorithms across self-driving platforms. Close collaboration with software, hardware, and systems/safety teams; resolve onboard error and performance problems.
- ML Infrastructure: accelerator to the ML-first autonomy strategy. Distributed training platform, ML compiler, model component libraries; optimize on-cloud training and onboard inference.
- Simulation: build the simulator and simulation frameworks, sensor simulation, scenario generation, combining real-world data with synthetic techniques at scale.
- Technical Infrastructure: generic compute platform for mission-critical workflows (data processing and simulation), storage management for hundreds of PB, cloud infrastructure as IaaC, engineering productivity (build and CI/CD).

About You
- Current BS or MS candidate in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, or related field graduating December 2026 or later.
- Experience in one or more of: backend API design, applications development, large-scale distributed systems; data storage and processing systems; advanced algorithms using C++ and Python; machine learning, multithreading; x86 architecture; software performance tuning and optimization, robotics software frameworks, different compute modalities (CPU, GPU, FPGA), etc.
- Strong problem solving and programming skills.
