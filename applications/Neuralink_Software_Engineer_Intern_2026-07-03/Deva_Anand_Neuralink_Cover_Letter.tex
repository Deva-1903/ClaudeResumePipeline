Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Software Engineer Intern role on the Brain Interfaces Software Team. What draws me to Neuralink is that the software is the part that has to be right: the SDKs, verification, and monitoring that stand between a device and a person depending on it. I spent two years building production clinical software where reliability was the whole point, and I now do systems and testing-heavy engineering in my MS, so a team that owns the end-to-end stack for implant communication, verification, and monitoring is exactly where I want to contribute.

I try to understand systems from first principles. In my Performance Engineering coursework I optimized C++ matrix multiplication from a naive baseline through cache-aware tiling, manual AVX/SIMD vectorization, register-aware micro-kernels, and OpenMP, profiling L1/L3/TLB behavior with perf and reading the generated assembly to confirm the compiler did what I intended. Earlier I built a reverse-mode autodiff engine from scratch and validated it against JAX. I default to this: if I cannot rebuild a piece of a system myself, I do not trust that I understand it. I work day to day in Python and C/C++ on Linux and pick up unfamiliar stacks quickly.

I also have a real feel for what production-grade means, as opposed to research-grade, because I have been the person paged when the production one breaks. At Wysa I owned a multi-tenant NHS clinical backend serving 8+ clinical clients, added integration tests for regression protection, monitored production logs, and debugged tenant-specific incidents. My projects lean the same way: AgenticSearch is a service I hardened with 150+ automated tests and cell-level provenance, and AutoEval is an automated verification-tooling agent with guardrails against fooling its own metrics. Verification and monitoring software is exactly the kind of work I gravitate toward.

The line in your description about delivering reliable solutions rather than perfecting designs resonates with how I already work. I feel the urgency in the mission, and a role where software directly helps restore capability to people is the kind of stakes I want. I would be glad to bring first-principles engineering and a bias for shipping to the team.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
